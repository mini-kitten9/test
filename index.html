This is a siamese kitten that you are looking at.
They are very vocal, social, extroverted, and high energy!
The author's favorite cat!

Fun Fact:
A cat's sensitive whiskers are at the same width as its body, so it uses these to measure if it can fit inside something, such as a hole.

What you are looking at is an orange cat.
They are very food-motivated and are highly energetic, affectionate, and vocal.
They have orange fur with a tabby pattern.
Goofy orange cats!

Fun Fact:
Cats love small cardboard boxes or enclosed spaces, because it helps them feel safe.

This is a calico cat you are seeing here. They are female, independent, loyal, vocally expressive, and highly energetic!
They have a patch-like tri-coat pattern, and calico is not a breed.
A cute little 3-in-1 cat!

Fun Fact:
Cats communicate more with other cats using their body language more than meowing, as meowing is a language developed partially for humans!

This is what we call a tuxedo cat.
These cats are often intelligent, attached, vocal, and playful!
Tuxedo is a coat pattern, not a breed.
Cute monochrome cats!

Fun Fact:
A cat can hear sounds from 4-5 times farther than a normal human, and hears 3 times better! The frequency range that cats can hear is significantly larger than humans.
